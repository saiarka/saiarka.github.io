Starting with a blank page

Every now and then, an idea sticks around long enough that it deserves its own place to live.

I’ve wanted a personal blog for a while—not because every thought needs to be published, but because writing can be a good way to understand what I think. This can be a quiet place to collect those thoughts without worrying about an audience or a schedule.

The first version can be simple. A few HTML pages, readable text, and room to grow when there’s a reason to grow.
